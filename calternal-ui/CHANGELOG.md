# Changelog

## 1.0.0 (2026-11-15)

First release (formerly `calternal-frame`). Calternal's look for bb, after Craft's window.

- **Window:** your wallpaper behind flat, tinted sidebars; blurred once, not on every repaint.
- **Chat card:** floating glass with a soft two-layer shadow, flush with the window top and bottom, the iPhone's corner radius. Wide by default: your messages on the right with iMessage tails, the agent's from the left.
- **Light and dark:** surfaces stay light or dark whatever the wallpaper; all text measured to WCAG 4.5:1.
- **Accent:** one colour, from the wallpaper or your own, drives bb's palette.
- **Composer:** one row of icons (model, context ring, permission, environment); one line on phones until you type; voice input as an accent pill with a clock.
- **Sidebar:** compact navigation aligned with the thread list (it follows bb's Customize navigation); `+` and search in the footer; More and the footer's … appear when you hover near them. The table-of-contents rail sits on the left and appears on hover.
- **Details:** warm tooltips, glass menus, dialogs and hover cards, even 12px spacing under every message, a 24-hour clock (configurable), status strips moved out of the chat (they are in the right sidebar).
- **Phones:** swipe a thread to archive or pin, swipe a message to reply or see its time, pull down to search, a keyboard-down button, attachment chip.
- **Settings:** wallpaper library with Unsplash, Google Fonts stored locally, glass strength, interface size, and Storage: every stored wallpaper and font with its size, removable.
