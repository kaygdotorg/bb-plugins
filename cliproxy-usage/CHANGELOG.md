# Changelog

## 1.0.0 (2026-11-15)

First release.

- **Footer:** pooled Claude and Codex usage as coloured percentages in place of the chart icon (amber and red thresholds measured for contrast in both themes). A click opens the per-account breakdown.
- **Settings:** footer on or off, window (session, weekly, or session then weekly), providers, icons or names, amber and red thresholds.
- **RPC:** `usage_read` (every account) and `usage_summary` (pooled per provider) for other plugins.
- The CLIProxyAPI management key stays on the bb server.
